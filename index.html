Welcome!

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at McMaster University, I will be on the 2027-28 academic job market. My research interests are in Quantitative Macroeconomics, International Finance, and Business Cycles, with a focus on sovereign debt, public capital, and investment dynamics.

My job market paper, “Sovereign Default, Public Capital and Time to Build,” studies how time-to-build frictions in public investment interact with sovereign default risk. My work combines quantitative structural models with computational methods to study macro-development and international finance questions relevant for policy.
